For Ibrahim

The winter frost marks the semester’s end,
A hard-fought war where broken spirits mend.
The halls of Erwin’s class are left behind,
No more unfair grades to cloud the mind.
The ink has dried on every taxing page,
As Ibrahim steps off the student stage.
The greatest gift that luck could ever bring,
Is silence where the school bells used to ring.
So raise a glass to freedom, bright and new,
A happy, Erwin-free birthday to you!
