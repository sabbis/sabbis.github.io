Crazy Way — Update 1.1 Release Notes

New Mode: Maze
A brand-new game mode is now available: Maze. Players can explore a gigantic maze to collect trophies and gems, with 140 seconds to escape through the portal. If the portal is not entered in time, the run ends with 0 gems and negative trophies. Players can now flag locations inside the maze and return to them later using a directional arrow. Maze mode unlocks at 150 trophies after being claimed on Trophy Road and requires Tickets to play.

New Currency: Tickets
Tickets are a new currency used to enter Maze mode. They can be unlocked through Trophy Road or purchased in the Shop for 20 gems.

Shop & Profile Changes
The Shop is now live and replaces the Profile icon. The Profile UI has been moved to Settings. After updating, players can also claim a one-time mystery gift in the Shop.

Trophy Road Expansion
Trophy Road has been expanded up to 7,600 trophies with the addition of two new leagues: Sapphire and Ruby. Trophy progression has been made easier overall. A full Trophy Road explanation video is planned for January.

New Worlds
Two new worlds have been added and can be unlocked through Trophy Road: Royal and City. No new modifiers were introduced in this update.

New Skins
New skins have been added to the game. Rare skins include Black and Crowned, Epic introduces Roads, and Mythic skins include Royal and Skyscraper. These skins are not obtainable from the Claw Machine. More information will be shared later in January.

UI & Fixes
Several areas of the UI have been cleaned up and improved. This update also includes multiple bug fixes and performance improvements.
